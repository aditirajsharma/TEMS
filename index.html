Our Focus

Conference Theme

"Innovating for All: AI, Sustainability, and the Spirit of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam"

This conference theme embodies the holistic vision of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — the ancient Indian philosophy that the world is one family — by emphasising inclusive innovation, responsible technology and global collaboration. In an era shaped by Artificial Intelligence (AI), the theme explores how intelligent systems can support societies, sustainability and academic development across borders.

The conference highlights the transformative role of AI in addressing global challenges, including climate resilience, healthcare accessibility, innovation governance, and the development of ethical digital ecosystems. It provides a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, scientists, engineers, business leaders, industry leaders and students to exchange cutting-edge ideas, showcase emerging technologies and present impactful research.

Featuring keynote sessions, panel discussions and invited talks by eminent experts, the conference aims to inspire collaboration around innovation grounded in ethics, sustainability and the shared responsibility of building a better future for all. Original research papers and impactful project contributions are invited.

About the Conference

About the Conference

The 2027 IEEE 6th International Conference on Technology, Engineering, Management for Societal Impact using Marketing, Entrepreneurship, and Talent continues the prestigious global conference series of the Technology and Engineering Management Society (TEMS), serving as the annual flagship platform for thought leadership in technology, innovation, and engineering management. Since its inception in 2020, the conference series has consistently brought together researchers, practitioners, and professionals from around the world to explore the evolving intersections of technological advancement and strategic management practices.

The 6th edition embraces the transformative conference theme "Innovating for All: AI, Sustainability, and the Spirit of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam", reflecting a vision of inclusive innovation, responsible technological growth, and global unity. This edition will explore how technology and AI can drive sustainable development and humanity-centric values that are shaping the future of technology management.

The conference invites original research, case studies, and innovative solutions that present interdisciplinary approaches, novel frameworks, implementation models, ethical considerations, and global best practices. Contributions that demonstrate how innovation and AI can drive world-class technical education in industries, societies, and nations are especially encouraged.

The 2027 IEEE 6th International Conference aims to foster collaborative learning, inspire impactful research, and promote a shared commitment to advancing technology for the benefit of all — truly echoing the spirit of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam.

Attendees will benefit from a rich programme featuring:

  • Plenary sessions by distinguished global experts
  • Invited talks on AI, sustainability, governance and technology management
  • High-quality research papers selected through rigorous peer review
  • Networking opportunities for academicians, researchers, industry leaders, students, entrepreneurs, and policy thinkers
  • Proceedings indexed in IEEE Xplore Digital Library
